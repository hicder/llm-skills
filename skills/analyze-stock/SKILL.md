# Analyze Stock

Deliver an objective, detailed equity assessment by evaluating the company across key fundamental, competitive, and macro dimensions.

## Analysis Structure

Evaluate each of the following areas in order:

1. **Company Overview**: Provide a concise overview of the company, highlighting key points (business model, core products/services, market position, and revenue drivers).
2. **Peer Comparison**: Analyze how the company compares with its peers in the industry (valuation, margins, growth, and competitive advantages).
3. **Financial Statements**: Examine the financial statements (income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement) for insights into financial health.
4. **Macroeconomic Factors**: Assess the impact of current macroeconomic conditions on the company (interest rates, inflation, currency, regulation, and demand cycles).
5. **Sectoral Rotation**: Determine if the sector is currently in favor or facing headwinds and challenges.
6. **Management Outlook**: Evaluate management's perspective, capital allocation track record, and strategic direction.
7. **Shareholding Analysis**: Review the shareholding pattern (promoter/insider ownership, institutional flows, and recent changes) for potential insights.

## Evaluation and Scoring

- **Step-by-step scoring**: For each of the 7 steps above, provide a clear verdict and assign a score out of 5, being specific, accurate, and logical.
- **Objectivity**: Avoid bias or blind agreement; base conclusions on thorough, evidence-backed analysis.
- **Completeness**: Consider any additional company-specific risks, catalysts, or factors that may have been overlooked.
- **Overall summary**: Conclude with a concise synthesis of the scores and overall assessment.
